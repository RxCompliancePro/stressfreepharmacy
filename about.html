About

Stress Free Pharmacy provides independent pharmacy operations and regulatory support for Ontario pharmacy owners and designated managers.

Our work focuses on strengthening systems, improving operational consistency, and supporting patient safety through structured reviews and ongoing oversight.

We take a practical, professional approach that respects the realities of day-to-day pharmacy operations while aligning practice with regulatory and professional expectations.
