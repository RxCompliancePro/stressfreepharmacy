An independent pharmacy compliance and operational advisory firm specializing in structured oversight, regulatory risk mitigation, and implementation-focused practice improvement systems.

Our Mandate

RXCELSIOR SOLUTIONS provides independent, structured oversight designed to strengthen pharmacy compliance with OCP and NAPRA standards while improving operational consistency across all practice domains.

We focus on identifying regulatory and operational risk, translating compliance requirements into practical systems, and ensuring pharmacies maintain a continuous state of inspection readiness.

Our Approach

Our methodology combines structured evaluation, gap analysis, and direct implementation support. We do not stop at identifying deficiencies — we develop the operational systems required to correct them.

This includes SOP design, workflow engineering, documentation systems, training frameworks, and quality improvement structures tailored to real-world pharmacy operations.
