About Rxcelsior Solutions Inc.

Supporting pharmacies with operational oversight, regulatory alignment, and patient safety without adding extra stress to owners or managers.

Our Mission

We help pharmacy owners and designated managers maintain consistent, high-quality operations. Our goal is to reduce errors, improve workflow efficiency, and support strong professional practices across all staff, without taking away control from leadership.

Our Approach

We provide independent operational reviews and ongoing oversight. By identifying gaps, recommending practical improvements, and monitoring compliance over time, we ensure pharmacies run smoothly, safely, and in alignment with regulatory standards.

Why Choose Us

  • Reduce operational drift and maintain workflow consistency
  • Professional oversight without managing staff directly
  • Support for regulatory compliance and patient safety
  • External perspective and actionable recommendations for continuous improvement
  • Tailored programs for pharmacies of all sizes and operational maturity

Our Experience

Our lead consultant brings 7 years of hands-on pharmacy management experience, including:

  • Non-sterile and sterile compounding operations
  • Ontario Drug Benefit (ODB) audits for both non-sterile and sterile compounding
  • Full Ontario College of Pharmacists (OCP) reviews
  • Starting and managing a successful non-sterile compounding business within a pharmacy

Our team provides actionable, practical guidance, giving pharmacy owners confidence that operations are compliant, safe, and efficient.

All engagements are confidential. Documentation may be securely deleted upon request once services are completed.
