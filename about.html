Our Mandate

RXCELSIOR SOLUTIONS provides structured pharmacy oversight aligned with OCP and NAPRA standards, focused on strengthening operational control, regulatory compliance, and inspection readiness.

We work with pharmacy owners and operators to translate regulatory expectations into practical, enforceable systems that reduce variability, mitigate risk, and ensure consistent execution across daily operations.

The objective is not temporary compliance, but the establishment of durable systems that sustain performance under real-world conditions.

Our Approach

Our work begins with structured evaluation across key operational and compliance domains, identifying gaps in workflows, documentation, and accountability systems.

From there, we develop and implement targeted solutions — including SOP frameworks, workflow redesign, and compliance tracking systems — designed to correct deficiencies and prevent recurrence.

Engagements are execution-focused, ensuring that corrective actions are not only defined, but embedded into daily operations.

Positioning

RXCELSIOR SOLUTIONS operates as an independent advisory partner, providing external oversight and objective assessment across pharmacy operations.

This independence enables clear identification of risks, unbiased evaluation of systems, and direct alignment with regulatory expectations.

The result is a structured, accountable, and inspection-ready operating environment.
