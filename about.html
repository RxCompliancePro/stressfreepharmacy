Our Mandate

RXCELSIOR SOLUTIONS provides independent oversight and structured operational guidance to support pharmacies in maintaining full compliance with OCP and NAPRA expectations.

Our focus is on reducing regulatory risk, improving operational consistency, and ensuring pharmacies remain continuously inspection-ready through practical, enforceable systems.

Our Approach

We operate through structured evaluation frameworks, targeted gap identification, and direct implementation support designed to translate regulatory requirements into practical daily operations.

Every engagement is designed to move beyond assessment into execution — ensuring identified issues are resolved through SOPs, workflows, training systems, and measurable compliance tools.
