The Reality

Your Dispensing Pharmacy Is Not Your Compliance Partner.

Most retirement homes rely on their dispensing pharmacy as their primary medication resource. But your dispensing pharmacy has a commercial interest in your prescriptions — not an independent obligation to your regulatory standing.

An RHRA inspection evaluates whether your systems, documentation, and staff practices meet the standard. That gap — between having a pharmacy supplier and having compliant, optimized medication systems — is exactly where citations, corrective action orders, and licence risk accumulate.

We fill that gap as your independent pharmacist — available when you need guidance, present monthly to keep your systems sharp, and accountable to your residents' safety above everything else.

What's at Stake
⚠
Licence Risk

RHRA findings are documented and can escalate to conditions placed directly on your operating licence.

⚠
Public Inspection Reports

RHRA results are publicly posted. Compliance failures are visible to prospective residents, families, and the media.

⚠
Medication Error Liability

Errors occurring without documented independent oversight create significant legal and regulatory exposure for ownership.
