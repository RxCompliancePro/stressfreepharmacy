Comprehensive Service Scope

  • Full 15-Section Pharmacy Audit: A deep-dive assessment covering all functional domains including SOPs, narcotics management, and clinical workflows.
  • 12-Section NAPRA Compounding Review: Detailed evaluation of non-sterile compounding practices, logs, and environments to ensure safety and compliance.
  • Metric-Based Compliance Scoring: We apply "Master Scores" to each section, providing a quantitative baseline of your regulatory standing.
  • Visual Performance Reporting: Findings are summarized using automated charts and gauges for clear, high-level oversight.
  • Prioritized Gap Analysis: Identification of critical risks with a ranked list of implementation priorities to resolve deficiencies quickly.
  • SOP & Template Provision: We provide the actual written operating procedures and clinical templates needed to satisfy OCP and NAPRA requirements.
  • Inspection Readiness: Direct alignment with official standards to remove the stress and uncertainty from upcoming College assessments.
  • Practice Optimization: Implementation of efficient clinical logic to reduce errors and drive pharmacy practice excellence.
