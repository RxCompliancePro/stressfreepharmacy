Recovery & Remediation Scope

Inspection Findings Assessment
Detailed review of OCP inspection reports to classify deficiencies, risks, and required corrective actions.
Immediate Risk Stabilization
Rapid identification and correction of high-risk compliance issues impacting patient safety or regulatory status.
Corrective Action Planning
Structured remediation roadmap with prioritized tasks aligned to regulatory expectations and timelines.
SOP & Documentation Repair
Revision and reconstruction of missing or non-compliant standard operating procedures and records.
Workflow & Practice Correction
Adjustment of operational workflows to eliminate repeat deficiencies and reinforce compliant practice behavior.
Regulatory Re-Alignment (OCP & NAPRA)
Full recalibration of pharmacy operations to meet provincial and national regulatory standards.
Re-Inspection Readiness
Preparation and validation of corrective actions to ensure successful follow-up inspection outcomes.
