Non-Sterile Compounding Compliance & Excellence Review

A structured, risk-based evaluation of non-sterile compounding practices, focused on regulatory alignment, documentation integrity, and quality system performance.

Contact Us

Overview

This service delivers a comprehensive review of non-sterile compounding operations, ensuring alignment with NAPRA standards while strengthening safety, documentation, and quality control systems.

Scope of Work

Risk Class A, B & C Compounding Assessment

Evaluation of compounding practices across all risk categories to ensure safety, consistency, and compliance.

SOP & Procedure Framework Review

Assessment and refinement of compounding procedures aligned with NAPRA requirements.

Training & Competency Systems

Review of staff training programs, competency validation, and ongoing qualification processes.

Quality Improvement Program (QIP)

Implementation and evaluation of structured quality monitoring and continuous improvement systems.

Compounding Documentation Systems

Review of formulation records, logs, and traceability systems to ensure documentation integrity.

Environmental & Workflow Controls

Assessment of workspace setup, process flow, and contamination risk mitigation practices.

Regulatory Gap Identification & Correction

Identification of compliance deficiencies with prioritized corrective action planning.

Methodology

Structured compounding audit combining risk classification review, documentation analysis, and workflow evaluation aligned with NAPRA model standards.

Deliverables

Detailed compliance report, SOP recommendations, documentation frameworks, and prioritized remediation plan.

Outcomes

Strengthened compounding compliance, improved documentation integrity, and reduced regulatory risk across all non-sterile compounding activities.
