Overview

This service provides an accelerated compliance review for pharmacies requiring immediate evaluation and corrective direction under time-sensitive conditions.

Scope of Work

Rapid Regulatory Audit

Condensed but comprehensive evaluation of key operational and compliance domains.

Critical Risk Identification

Immediate detection of high-risk issues requiring urgent mitigation.

Priority Gap Analysis

Ranking of compliance deficiencies based on severity and regulatory exposure.

Compounding Compliance Review

Targeted assessment of compounding practices and documentation where applicable.

Fast-Track SOP & Documentation Review

Rapid evaluation of documentation systems and identification of critical gaps.

Inspection Readiness Assessment

Evaluation of preparedness for imminent OCP inspection.

Immediate Action Recommendations

Clear, prioritized actions designed for rapid implementation and stabilization.

Methodology

Accelerated assessment framework focused on high-impact risk areas, enabling rapid identification and correction of compliance deficiencies.

Deliverables

Priority action plan, risk summary, and targeted compliance recommendations for immediate execution.

Outcomes

Stabilized operations, reduced regulatory risk, and improved readiness for imminent inspection or audit events.
