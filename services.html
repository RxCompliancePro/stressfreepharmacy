Our Services

Structured programs to strengthen pharmacy operations, ensure compliance, and maintain consistency.

Pharmacy Operations & Regulatory Review

Full review of pharmacy operations aligned with OCP standards, including workflow assessment, documentation checks, and patient safety evaluation. Written report with prioritized recommendations included.

Ongoing Operations Oversight Program

Quarterly reviews to maintain operational stability, prevent drift, and ensure consistent compliance. Includes written summaries, billing audits, and trend tracking.

Compounding Practice Review

Assessment of non-sterile and sterile compounding practices. Recommendations focus on safety, documentation, and regulatory defensibility.

Biannual Compounding Oversight

Twice-yearly oversight of compounding services to monitor consistency, minimize risk, and maintain high-quality operations.

Specialized Consulting Services

Implementation-focused support including SOP development, workflow redesign, staff training, master formulation support, and other services. Scope and pricing agreed in advance.

All engagements are confidential. Documentation may be securely deleted upon request once services are completed.
